Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Untitled Advertising and Untitled Adflux Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), an out-of-home advertising business based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India, collect, use, and protect information when you contact us — including through WhatsApp — and when our team uses our internal business application.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact & enquiry details you share with us: name, business name, phone number, email, and the content of your messages.
  • WhatsApp messages you send to our business number, received via the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta), so our team can respond to your enquiry.
  • Lead & deal records our sales team creates to follow up on enquiries (e.g. requirement, city, status).
  • Operational data of our own staff (e.g. attendance, location while on duty, call logs) used solely to run our business. This concerns our employees, not customers.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotations and services.
  • To manage leads, follow-ups, and customer relationships.
  • To send you information you have requested or agreed to receive.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our internal business systems.

3. WhatsApp messaging

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform to receive and reply to customer messages. We message you only in response to your enquiry or where you have opted in. We do not sell your WhatsApp number or message content. Your use of WhatsApp is also governed by WhatsApp's and Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate — for example Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), and our hosting and database providers — and only as needed to provide our services, or where required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop messaging you, at any time by contacting us below. To stop WhatsApp messages, reply STOP or tell us you wish to opt out.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.
