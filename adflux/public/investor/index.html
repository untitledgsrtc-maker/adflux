The asset — and the gap

Every screen can run 5,040 ads a day. We run 100.

A standard booking fills a screen with 100 ten-second slots a day — out of a 5,040-slot capacity. Measured across our won LED deals, real occupancy is 1.98%. The asset exists; there is 50× headroom on every screen we already own.

Sold per screen: 2% (100 / 5,040 daily slots)Empty: 98% upside

Growth here is not a capacity problem — the screens are built. It's a demand problem. That's what we're funding.

The unlock

Why 2% — and what takes it to 10%.

We're at 2% because the government rate card (DAVP pricing) for these screens is still being finalised. Departments can't book at scale until the price is set. The moment DAVP prices land, the government demand already flowing through GSRTC can be booked against our screens — at ₹1 per slot, five times the private per-slot rate.

Stated plainly

What has to be true — and how to reach us.

  • DAVP timing. The thesis assumes DAVP pricing lands and govt demand follows within ~6 months. If it slips, fill ramps slower.
  • Fill is demand-led. The screens exist; the ad-time has to be sold. The plan funds the sales team precisely for this.
  • Government concentration & cyclicality. Most revenue is govt and election-cyclical; private LED is early. We're diversifying.
  • Unaudited accounts. FY24-25 & FY25-26 audits and entity consolidation are in progress — a condition precedent to funding.
