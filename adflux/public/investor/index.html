What we are

Measured LED advertising screens inside Gujarat's GSRTC bus stations.

264 screens across 20 GSRTC bus stations, selling ad-time to government departments (via DAVP) and private brands. AI cameras at each screen count real impressions and audience — so advertisers buy proven reach on a captive commuter, not an estimate on a painted board.

Captive attention

A commuter waiting at a bus station, phone down, with minutes to spare — the one audience Google and Meta can't reach.

Measured, not estimated

Every screen carries an AI camera reporting real impressions and demographics. We sell a count, not a guess.

Concession-locked

Screens sit inside GSRTC property under a multi-year concession. A rival can build next to a station — not inside it.

How it works

A screen you can measure — not a wall you rent.

Play

Ten-second ad slots run on LED screens inside the stations. Each screen holds 5,040 slots a day, scheduled by software.

Measure

An AI camera on every screen counts who actually looked — real impressions and audience, not a traffic estimate.

Prove

A QR code turns a viewer into a scan, and a scan into a lead. The advertiser sees the response, not a rate card.

A hoarding cannot tell you who saw it. Our screen can — that is the line between renting space and selling attention.

Who is watching

A captive audience a roadside hoarding never gets.

  • A commuter at a bus station is waiting — 10 to 40 minutes, phone in hand, little else to look at.
  • A roadside hoarding gets three seconds from a moving car. Our screen gets minutes from a person going nowhere.
  • Dwell time is the whole game in outdoor advertising — and bus stations are one of the few places it genuinely exists.

The unlock

Why 2% — and what takes it to 10%.

We're at 2% because the government rate card (DAVP pricing) for these screens is still being finalised — departments can't book at scale until the price is set. When DAVP prices land, the government demand already flowing through GSRTC can be booked against our screens, at ₹1 per slot — five times the private per-slot rate.

The concession

Exclusive rights on infrastructure no competitor can rebuild.

  • GSRTC is Gujarat's state road transport corporation. Its bus stations are public infrastructure — nobody can put a second one next door.
  • Our screens sit inside that infrastructure under a multi-year concession. The relationship is the barrier, not the hardware.
  • Government is also a customer here — departments advertise on the same screens through DAVP. We sit on both sides of the desk.

Stated plainly

What has to be true — the honest read.

  • DAVP timing. The 5× rests on the government rate card being notified. We don't control the date and won't pretend one; a delay compounds slower, it doesn't zero the asset.
  • Fill is demand-led. The screens exist; the ad-time has to be sold. The plan funds a sales team precisely for this.
  • Government concentration. One buyer, one rate mechanism, one state concession. The cushion is a recurring public-information budget — and we're building a private second leg (still small at ₹5.76 L).
  • Payback needs fill. At 2%, the 20-station LED unit makes ~₹1.8 L/mo — not enough to service ₹5 Cr. It stands on its own near the 10% target. That is the bet we're asking you to underwrite.
  • Unaudited accounts. FY24-25 & FY25-26 audits and entity consolidation are in progress — a condition precedent to funding.
