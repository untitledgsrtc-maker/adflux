The concession

Exclusive rights on infrastructure no competitor can rebuild.

  • GSRTC is Gujarat's state road transport corporation. Its bus stations are public infrastructure — nobody can put a second one next door.
  • Our screens sit inside that infrastructure under a multi-year concession. The relationship is the barrier, not the hardware.
  • Government is also a customer here — departments advertise on the same screens through DAVP. We sit on both sides of the desk.

Stated plainly

What has to be true — the honest read.

  • DAVP timing. The 5× rests on the government rate card being notified. We don't control the date and won't pretend one; a delay compounds slower, it doesn't zero the asset.
  • Fill is demand-led. The screens exist; the ad-time has to be sold. The plan funds a sales team precisely for this.
  • Government concentration. One buyer, one rate mechanism, one state concession. The cushion is a recurring public-information budget — and we're building a private second leg (still small at ₹5.76 L).
  • Payback needs fill. At 2%, the 20-station LED unit makes ~₹1.8 L/mo — not enough to service ₹5 Cr. It stands on its own near the 10% target. That is the bet we're asking you to underwrite.
  • Unaudited accounts. FY24-25 & FY25-26 audits and entity consolidation are in progress — a condition precedent to funding.
