The honest math

On LED rates alone, the 98 stations don't pay back inside the contract. We'll say it out loud.

The uncomfortable number: at today's fill and LED rates, the new network matures to roughly ₹1 Cr/year of operating profit — and its ₹6.1 Cr build takes longer than the 5-year term to recover on LED alone. Auto-hood, our real profit engine, is a separate business and is not granted by this tender. So we don't hide behind it.

So why do it? Three reasons — and the calculator below lets you test every one of them yourself:

1 · Fill the empty inventory

We sell 2% of our slots. Every point of fill is almost pure margin — the screens already exist. Move the "fill" slider below and watch the forecast turn. This is the whole thesis: convert unused screen-time into private-brand demand.

2 · Lock the concession (the moat)

Winning grabs a multi-year exclusive on Gujarat's busiest transit hubs. The barriers that make it hard to win are the same ones that make it hard to take from us. The network itself is the asset.

3 · Scale the auto-hood engine

5× more stations is 5× the canvas for the bus-branding business that already nets ₹1.8 Cr/year — even where those rights are negotiated separately.

The revenue plan

How we get fill off 2%.

The screens are the cheap part. The plan puts the money into demand:

Private-brand sales team

Reps selling empty screen-time directly to local + national brands — the higher-margin demand. We already run a CRM built for exactly this pipeline.

Agency & programmatic tie-ups

Media agencies fill inventory in bulk — lower margin, real volume, fast to light up.

Government (DAVP) volume

Lean harder on the campaigns that already anchor the network and pay reliably.

Live AI-based audience analytics — impressions and demographics per screen. Proof we can report what advertisers buy.

Straight about the gap: the tender's true upfront need is ~₹9.5 Cr, but most of that isn't a hard spend — the ₹80 L EMD is refundable, the bank guarantee is an instrument (not cash), and the build is phased to GSRTC's batch handover, not paid on day one. Equity + a receivables-backed facility + internal cash cover it — provided the bank facility lands. We don't pretend ₹5 Cr alone does it.

Why equity, not all debt

The upfront EMD, guarantee, and the Year-1 revenue ramp can't be debt-serviced from day one. Equity absorbs the launch; debt funds the self-liquidating receivables.

What you're backing

A cash-generating operator with a 10-year record and a government-anchored moat — making a measured, staged bet on a 5× network. Set your own valuation in the calculator above; the terms are a conversation.

Assumptions & risks — stated plainly

What has to be true.

  • Government concentration. Most revenue is government today. We're diversifying into private LED and private-brand auto-hood.
  • Election cyclicality. Peak years coincide with election spend. The forecast uses conservative, off-peak run-rates.
  • Fill is demand-led. We sell ~2% of inventory. The upside is real but has to be sold — the plan funds a private-brand sales team.
  • Auto-hood attribution. The forecast's "total" leans on auto-hood, which is a separate business. Whether its rights extend to the 98 new stations is being confirmed pre-close.
  • Capital intensity. LED payback is long; auto-hood shortens the blended picture. Equity is sized to absorb the Year-1 ramp.
  • Unaudited accounts. FY24-25 & FY25-26 audits and the two-entity consolidation are in progress — a condition precedent to funding.
