01
Principles
Five rules that decide every design call. When in doubt, re-read these.
1
One primary action per screen.
 The single most important thing the user should do is the biggest, the boldest, and the only yellow button on the screen. Everything else is secondary (outline) or tertiary (text).
2
Money is sacred.
 Rupee amounts, pipeline totals, incentives and scores get 
Space Grotesk
 at 22–42px. They are always the loudest number in any card.
3
Mobile is the default.
 Field reps live on their phones. Design for the thumb first: primary actions in the bottom third, identity/status up top, 44px minimum tap targets.
4
Status uses tokens, never new colors.
 Green = good, amber = attention, red = urgent, blue = info, purple = AI/voice. Don't invent hues.
5
Every screen earns a hero.
 A teal hero card anchors each screen with the one number that matters there — today's revenue, score, SLA, queue size.
02
