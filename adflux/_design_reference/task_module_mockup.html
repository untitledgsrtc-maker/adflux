Why this matters more than it looks
A won hoarding order makes a handful of jobs, once.

This client makes 12 jobs every month, and keeps making them. Across a few retainer clients that is most of what the creative team does — and none of it comes from a quote.
The second decision. Client sign-off is a different gate from ours. Renuka checks the design; then the client has to approve it before it goes out. The two yellow steps above are that gate. They belong on digital work only — putting them on a hoarding job adds two clicks that mean nothing.
