5. First run consent (once)

Before you start✕
While your timer runs, this app records
  • the program and window title you are in
  • when you are idle
  • a screenshot every 10 minutes
Nothing is recorded when the timer is stopped. Admin and your team head can see it. Screenshots are deleted after 30 days.

6. Tray icon (bottom right)

Grey: signed in, no timer
Green: timer running
Amber: idle question waiting
Red: signed out or offline over 1 hour
Right-click: Start work, Stop timer, Open Untitled Work, Open in browser, Sign out, Quit.

7. Rules

  • Windows 10 and 11, Tauri, about 10 MB, starts with Windows, unsigned installer (More info, then Run anyway, once per PC).
  • Same login and same timer functions as the website, so web, app and Team Work always agree.
  • Records only while a timer runs. No keystrokes, no mouse positions, no file contents.
  • Offline: keeps recording, uploads later (up to 48 hours). Auto-stop 21:30 IST as on the web.
  • Admin and team head see it in Team Work, new Activity tab. Tracked people switched on one by one (Safika first).
  • Later rule, not in this build: minimum 8 hours a day.
