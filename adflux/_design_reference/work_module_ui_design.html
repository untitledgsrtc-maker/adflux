Work module - UI design

Design only. Nothing is built. Timeclock + tasks (one-time and repeating, one or many people) + admin dashboard. Built with the ui-ux-pro-max rules on the app's own brand (navy and brand yellow, DM Sans and Space Grotesk, Lucide icons at stroke 1.6).

Rules applied: 16 px body text, 44 px or larger tap targets, 8 px or larger gaps, status = icon + word + colour, a visible focus ring, empty / loading / error states drawn, no emoji, motion off when the phone asks for less motion.

What the design deliberately does not have

No screenshots. No keyboard or mouse counts. No productivity percentage. No status the person has to remember to change. Hours do not affect salary, score or attendance.
