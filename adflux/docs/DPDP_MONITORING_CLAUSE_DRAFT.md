# Work monitoring notice - clause for the offer letter (DRAFT, owner to review with a lawyer)

Owner decision 15 Oct 2026: the notice is part of the offer letter and is accepted with it (no separate letter).
The Windows app's first-run consent screen stays as the technical record of acceptance on each PC.

Suggested clause (plain words):

> **Work activity recording.** While you have a work timer running on a company computer, the Untitled Work app
> records the name of the program and the window title you are using, the periods when you are idle, and a
> screenshot of your screen about every 10 minutes. Nothing is recorded when your timer is stopped. This is
> used only to verify working hours, to bill clients correctly and to review work, and is visible only to the
> company owner/administrator and your team head. Screenshots are deleted after 30 days. We do not record
> keystrokes, passwords or the content of your personal messages. By accepting this offer you consent to this
> recording for the purpose stated. You may ask the company for a copy of your recorded data.

Notes for the owner:
- Existing staff who already accepted an offer letter without this clause: have them sign a one-line acknowledgement; the in-app consent screen is stored with time and PC name in `work_agent_devices`.
- Keep the purposes list short and true; do not use the data for anything not named here.
